a sauceheavy tomato sauce

also: tomato-and-sugar sauce · sweet red sauce · thick tomato barbecue sauce · Kansas City-style sauce

'Sweet' or 'mild' on Upstate menus; 'barbecue sauce', without qualification, to most of the country.

A thick red sauce built on tomato — ketchup, paste or sauce — sweetened with brown sugar or molasses, soured with vinegar and seasoned with spices, cooked down until it clings. It is the sauce of the western and northwestern corner of South Carolina on the state's four-sauce map: the tourism office's 'tomato-and-sugar sauce', the one it says you are most likely to find on a grocery shelf. It goes on after cooking — on the plate, on the bun, on ribs and chicken — and sits on the meat rather than soaking in.

The story Cited · wp-barbecue-sauce

This is the national sauce at the state's edge. Heinz put out the first major bottled barbecue sauce in 1940, General Foods' Open Pit followed, Kraft came in around 1960 and advertised its way to first place, and after the war the sugar and the ingredient count of sauces climbed; the typical commercial sauce is the Kansas City kind, 'thick, reddish-brown, tomato-based, and made with sugar, vinegar, and spices', which Wikipedia's sauce history places downstream of the Piedmont's thin dip and Memphis's sauce. The South Carolina Barbeque Association says the heavy tomato sauce 'evolved only recently, that is, in the last sixty or so years', pushed by commercial brands, and Wikipedia's South Carolina article calls it 'more similar to sauces popular in other states'. Inference — a sauce that arrived by bottle would settle where the older sauces were thinnest on the ground, at the state's western edge, farthest from the whole-hog coast and the German Midlands. Henry's Smokehouse in Greenville makes its own tomato-based mild sauce by hand and sets a mustard-based spicy one beside it.

How it is done Tradition

Simmer tomato with brown sugar or molasses, vinegar, salt, black pepper and spices until it thickens. It is a finishing and table sauce: brushed on ribs and chicken near the end of cooking or served alongside, spooned over chopped pork on the plate. It is the one Carolina sauce that coats rather than soaks.

Today Cited · web-henrys-destination-bbq

Henry's Smokehouse in Greenville serves its tomato-based mild sauce beside a mustard one; national bottles fill the western shelves. Greenwood's July Festival of Discovery judges Carolina barbecue and hash under Kansas City Barbeque Society rules.

Barbecue Sauce (Kansas City-style)

Wikibooks Cookbook · read 16 September 2026 · source · CC BY-SA 4.0
  • 1 cup (235 mL) ketchup
  • 6 tablespoons (90 mL) Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 tablespoons (60 mL) butter or margarine
  • 3 tablespoons (45 mL) white vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon (15 mL) plain prepared mustard
  • 3 tablespoons (45 mL) finely minced yellow onion
  • 4 teaspoons (20 mL) bottled liquid smoke flavor (hickory)
  • ¼ teaspoon (1 mL) Louisiana-style red pepper sauce
  • ½ cup (120 mL) brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon (15 mL) granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon (5 mL) salt
1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan. 2. Mix well. 3. Simmer over low heat for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Makes 8 servings.

The thick sweet style the up-country South Carolina sauces sit closest to: ketchup, brown sugar, Worcestershire, butter, a little vinegar, liquid smoke.

Barbecue Sauce (Sweet)

Wikibooks Cookbook · read 16 September 2026 · source · CC BY-SA 4.0
  • 1 cup tomato catsup (ketchup)
  • ¾ cup cider vinegar
  • 2–3 tablespoons mustard
  • 3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1–2 teaspoons celery seed
  • 3 tablespoons margarine
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon red pepper
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon onion flakes
  • Garlic powder or minced garlic to taste (optional)
1. Combine all ingredients together and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer 10 to 15 minutes, until it thickens slightly. 2. Arrange chicken pieces or pork chops in a greased baking dish. 3. Pour half of sauce over and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30 to 40 minutes. 4. Pour remaining sauce over before serving.

Barbecue Sauce II

Wikibooks Cookbook · read 16 September 2026 · source · CC BY-SA 4.0
  • 1 cup ketchup (catsup)
  • ½ cup molasses
  • ½ teaspoon "concentrated" liquid smoke flavoring
1. Mix everything in a pot. 2. To use, heat the sauce with some pre-cooked meat in it.

Three ingredients: ketchup, molasses, liquid smoke. The shortest way to the thick red end of the range.

Not to be confused with

  • light tomato sauce — Heavy tomato coats the meat and tastes of sugar; light tomato pours and tastes of vinegar.
  • Lexington dip — Both are red. Dip is thin and sour and soaks into the meat; heavy tomato sits on top of it.
