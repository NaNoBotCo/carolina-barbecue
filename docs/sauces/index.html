Carolina BarbecueSauces (15)

The sauces and dips that mark the map.

Heavy tomato (3)

Bone Suckin' Sauce

Bone Suckin' Sweet Southern, Ford's Foods sauce

A Raleigh line of bottled sauces, of which the one that built the brand is Sweet Southern: cane sugar first, tomato puree second, water third, apple cider vinegar fourth, at six grams of sugar to the tablespoon.

heavy tomato sauce

tomato-and-sugar sauce, sweet red sauce, thick tomato barbecue sauce

A thick red sauce built on tomato — ketchup, paste or sauce — sweetened with brown sugar or molasses, soured with vinegar and seasoned with spices, cooked down until it clings.

Sam Jones BBQ sauce

Sam Jones Sweet BBQ Sauce, Sam Jones Vinegar

A bottled line from the whole-hog house Sam Jones opened at Winterville in 2015, third generation of the Skylight Inn family at Ayden.

George's Barbecue Sauce

George's Sauce, George's Original, George's of Nashville

A bottled sauce from Nashville, North Carolina, a Nash County town on the edge of the Coastal Plain, sold in three strengths: Original, Hot and Special.

Light tomato (1)

light tomato sauce

light-tomato barbecue sauce, vinegar-and-ketchup sauce, thin red sauce

Vinegar-and-pepper sauce with ketchup added — enough to redden and sweeten it a little, not enough to thicken it — and often cayenne or a bottled hot sauce.

Mustard (4)

Golding Farms Foods

Golding Farms, Golding Blends

A Winston-Salem sauce manufacturer, the second of that city's two, and one whose output mostly reaches a table under someone else's name.

Maurice's Southern Gold

Southern Gold BBQ Sauce, Maurice's Gourmet Blend, Carolina Gold (former name)

The mustard sauce of Maurice's Piggie Park at West Columbia, sold in bottles, half gallons and gallons alongside the family's Hickory Red, Vinegar and Hot Pepper sauces.

Melvin's Golden Sauce

Melvin's Original Golden Secret, Golden Secret Sauce

The mustard sauce of Melvin's Bar-B-Que in Charleston, sold in 16-ounce glass bottles, half gallons and gallons, beside the house's Southern Red, Sweet Red and Vinegar sauces.

Carolina Treet

vinegar-and-pepper sauce

eastern sauce, Eastern North Carolina sauce, East Carolina sauce

Vinegar — cider or white — with salt, black pepper and crushed red pepper, and nothing else in its plainest form; some houses add sugar, a bottled hot sauce or lemon.
