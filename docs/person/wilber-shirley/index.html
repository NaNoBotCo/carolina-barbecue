a person · NCWilber Shirley

also: Wilberdean Shirley · Wilberdean 'Wilber' Shirley

Wilberdean 'Wilber' Shirley (1930–2021) learned barbecue at Griffin's in Goldsboro, bought the former Hill's Barbecue on U.S. 70 east of town in 1962 and put his name on it, and cooked split whole hogs over oak coals there for more than forty years. Wilber's fed presidents, governors and senators and, on a holiday weekend, up to 130 pigs. It closed in March 2019 over unpaid taxes; a group of customers bought it and reopened it in July 2020, while he lived. He died on April 5, 2021, at ninety.

The story Cited · web-uncpress-wilber-shirley

'I went to work at a place called Griffin's Barbecue... That's how I first learned to cook it,' he told John Shelton Reed and Dale Volberg Reed for Holy Smoke. In 1962 — July 24, 1961, by WRAL's obituary — he bought Hill's Barbecue on Highway 70 and renamed it. 'Split halves of whole hogs over oak coals in closed pits,' he said of his method: 'That grease hitting those coals makes the smoked flavor. You can't spray it on there, paint it on there, put it on there.' The pits ran overnight, from about ten at night to ten in the morning; in season the place cooked 100 to 125 pigs a week, and Our State counts 130 on a Labor Day weekend and dining rooms for more than 300. 'I work seven days a week,' he said. 'Some weeks I come in eight days a week.' A waitress had been there more than thirty years, a cook since before he bought the place. Presidents George H.W. Bush and Bill Clinton, Governors Terry Sanford and Jim Hunt and Senator Jesse Helms ate there. The restaurant was shut in March 2019 for nonpayment of taxes. Willis B. Underwood III, an insurance salesman who had eaten there all his life, gathered other regulars into Goldpit Partners, bought it, and reopened it in July 2020 for curbside orders: 'I just didn't want to see the smoke die.' Shirley, a longtime member of First Free Will Baptist Church in Goldsboro, died at a retirement community in Kinston on April 5, 2021. The Campaign for Real Barbecue quotes him on its wood page: cooking whole hogs over hardwood coals overnight 'is not easy or cheap, but it is the right way, and something we as a community take a lot of pride in'.

Today Cited · web-wilbersbbq

Dead: April 5, 2021, at Kinston, aged ninety. Wilber's Barbecue is open under Goldpit Partners at 4172 U.S. Highway 70 East, Goldsboro, cooking whole hogs over oak; his portrait hangs in the dining room.
