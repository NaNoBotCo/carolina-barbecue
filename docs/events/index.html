Both states (3)

fundraiser barbecue

plate sale, barbecue plate sale, church barbecue

The plate sale: a church, a volunteer fire department, a Ruritan club or a scout troop cooks pork — a whole hog or a stack of butts — and sells it by the plate on a Saturday to pay for a roof, a truck or a mission.

pig pickin'

pig picking, pig pickin, pig pull

A party at which a whole hog is cooked for the crowd and the crowd eats it off the pig — picked by hand from the split carcass, or chopped on a block beside it.

political barbecue

stump meeting, campaign barbecue, rally barbecue

The free feed a candidate or a party puts on to draw a crowd: a whole hog, or these days a pile of pork butts, cooked for whoever comes, with speeches or a rope line attached.
