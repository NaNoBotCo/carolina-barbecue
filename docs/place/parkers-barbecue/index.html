a place · NCParker's Barbecue

also: The Original Parker's Barbecue · Parker's · Parker's BBQ of Wilson

A large eastern barbecue house on U.S. 301 south of Wilson, opened in August 1946 by brothers Graham and Ralph Parker and their cousin Henry Parker Brewer, and owned since 1987 by Donald Williams, who started there in 1963, with partners Eric Lippard and Kevin Lamm. It cooks about 150 whole hogs and fries about 8,000 chickens a week for some 20,000 customers, on gas cookers the founders installed in the 1950s. The barbecue is chopped fine and dressed with vinegar and red pepper; young staff in white paper caps seat the room; cash or check only.

The story Cited · web-ourstate-parkers

The restaurant opened in August 1946 just off Highway 301 as a family destination for pit-cooked barbecue and fried chicken, the owners' history says. The day that set it on its feet came in 1954, when the Ralston Purina plant opened in Wilson and Parker's served 17,000 plates at 75 cents each in four hours. In the 1950s the cookers went from wood coals to gas, for a temperature that could be regulated. Donald Williams came to work in 1963 and bought the business when Graham and Ralph Parker retired in 1987. June Brunson worked there more than 62 years, until her death in 2020; Richard Ward, who came on opening day, was still a customer at 100 in 2013. Ralph Parker, the last of the founders, died on July 4, 2013 at 89. Williams and Kevin Lamm gave the Southern Foodways Alliance an oral history in November 2011, in which Lamm said the owners knew they were consistent because customers told them the place had not changed across decades. Three or four employees have been there more than forty years. On Mother's Day the kitchen uses 45 pigs and 26 chicken fryers.

How it is done Cited · web-ourstate-parkers

Hogs are dressed and cooked whole in a cinder-block building behind the restaurant, on gas cookers, and the meat is chopped fine and seasoned with a vinegar-and-red-pepper sauce. Barbecue is served from nine in the morning. Plates come with hushpuppies and slaw, and the menu runs to fried chicken, fish, string beans and, in season, oysters battered or stewed. Waiters in white caps seat and serve; the owners greet at the door.

Today Cited · web-parkersbbqwilson

Open seven days, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. for dine-in and to 7:30 for take-out, at 2514 U.S. Highway 301 South, Wilson, per the restaurant's site in September 2026. Cash or check; an ATM is on site. A separate Parker's operates in Greenville; the relationship is not documented here.
