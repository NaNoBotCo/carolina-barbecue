a wordbarbecue

also: bar-b-q · bar-b-que · barbeque · BBQ · 'cue · cue · Q

In the Carolinas the word is a noun for the cooked pork: you eat barbecue, you do not 'have a barbecue'. The grill in the yard is a grill; cooking on it is grilling.

The cooked meat itself — in the Carolinas, pork cooked slowly over coals or in a cooker and then chopped, pulled or sliced — and, by extension, the place that sells it and the gathering that eats it. Elsewhere in the country the same word means a grill, or a cookout of any meat; here the narrower sense is the ordinary one.

Root. Taíno (Arawakan) barbacoa, a framework of sticks set on posts → Spanish barbacoa (1526, Oviedo) → English barbecue (1660s: the frame and the method; the cooked animal and the gathering by the 1700s)
First seen. English 1661, Edmund Hickeringill's Jamaica Viewed ('Barbacu'd'), per the OED as quoted in the Wikipedia article; 'barbecado' in 1648; as a noun 1697, William Dampier. Spanish barbacoa printed by Gonzalo Fernández de Oviedo, 1526.
The folk etymology from French barbe à queue ('beard to tail', i.e. the whole animal) is a joke first printed in the eighteenth century and has no standing; the Taíno origin is the dictionary etymology. Spellings with -que and Bar-B-Q are twentieth-century sign-painting and are as common on Carolina signs as the dictionary form. Cited · wp-barbecue

The story Tradition

The word came north from the Caribbean with the Spanish and into English by way of the colonial Atlantic: a frame of sticks first, then the method, then the meat. Samuel Johnson's dictionary of 1755 already gives the Carolina sense — 'to barbecue: a term for dressing a whole hog', and the noun, 'a hog dressed whole'. Tradition holds — the whole-animal feast over wood coals was the shape of the colonial Southern gathering by the eighteenth century, and the political barbecue, a free feed put on by a candidate, was an institution of the early republic. Inference — the Carolina insistence that barbecue is a noun for pork is a memory of that feast: what was cooked at the rally was the pig, and the pig was what you came for.
