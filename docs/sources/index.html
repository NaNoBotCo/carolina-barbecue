    A white-authored book framed around 'Aunt Caroline', a Black cook drawn as a minstrel figure, with several recipe titles in that idiom; the recipes themselves are written plainly. Read in the Cornell University copy, which carries the Library's no-known-copyright-restrictions statement. Recipes quoted here keep the book's own titles and are chosen from the plainly named ones.

Read in the stereotype printing scanned by the Internet Archive as virginiahousewif00rand, whose page numbers are used here; the fourth-edition scan (virginiahousewi00rand, printed about 1830) was read alongside it to settle characters each scan loses.

    Abby Fisher was born into slavery in South Carolina about 1832 and dictated the book in San Francisco; her preface says she could neither read nor write. Recipes are numbered.
