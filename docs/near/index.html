Gaps

Thin so far: lgbtq+ welcoming (1), closes when sold out (2). The directories we read are listed on where all of this came from. A business that wants a tag it has earned can say so on its own site or in any of those directories.

Tags

✊🏾 Black-ownedthe owner's own statement, a press profile naming the owner, a Black-owned business directory, an SFA oral history
♀ Woman-ownedthe owner's own statement, a press profile naming the owner, a women-owned business certification (WBENC, WOSB)
🏳️‍🌈 LGBTQ+ welcomingthe business's own statement, a press list whose framing says the businesses in it are LGBTQ-owned, or a public directory (an LGBTQ chamber of commerce, a Pride business list, OpenStreetMap lgbtq=welcome)
🔥 Cooks over woodTrue 'Cue certification, the NCBS Historic Barbecue Trail, the SCBA, an SFA interview, a press piece describing the pit, the pit's own site
🐖 Whole hogwhat the pit says it cooks, or a press piece
👪 Family-runa press profile or the business's own history
💵 Cash onlythe business's own site or a dated press piece
⏳ Closes when sold outthe business's own site or a dated press piece
🍽 Buffetthe business's own site or a dated press piece
📅 Weekends onlythe business's own site or a dated press piece
🍚 Serves hasha menu or a press piece
⚫ Closeda dated press piece or the business's own notice

A tag names its evidence on the pit's own page. A missing tag says nothing about the pit, only about what we've read.
